# Pilot samples

Two end-to-end rehearsals of the registered authoring call, run against the
real model with the registered prompt in throwaway repositories before the
registered run (`DEVIATIONS.md` §1 explains why they exist and what they
cost). Retained because they are two more samples of the quantity this
study measures — which classes independently authored records reach — and
because discarding evidence the operator has already seen would be the
dishonest option.

They are NOT part of the registered result. They were produced outside the
protocol lock's publication and draw, so nothing here is beacon-selected
or transparency-logged. `ANALYSIS.md` reports them beside the registered
sample and says which is which.

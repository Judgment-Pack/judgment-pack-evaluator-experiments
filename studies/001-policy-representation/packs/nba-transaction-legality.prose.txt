NBA roster transaction legality under the 2023 CBA
==================================================

QUESTION: Is this set of NBA roster operations legal under the CBA?
INTENT: Determine whether a proposed set of NBA roster operations is legal under the encoded provisions of the Collective Bargaining Agreement, or must be escalated because a fact the encoded provisions depend on is unavailable.

POSSIBLE ANSWERS
* Legal (legal): No encoded CBA provision is violated on the facts supplied. This is the pack's fallback outcome: it is reached only when every rule evaluated to false, so it is never reached while any needed fact is unknown.
* Illegal (illegal): At least one encoded CBA provision is violated. Every rule in this pack is a violation detector and resolves to this outcome, so multiple simultaneous violations agree rather than conflict.
* If no numbered provision below is triggered, the answer is "legal".

HOW TO HANDLE A FACT YOU DO NOT HAVE
A fact that an encoded CBA provision depends on is unavailable, or the instance turns on a provision this pack does not encode. Review the trace: each unknown rule names the /facts/derived/* pointer it could not resolve, and each rule's sourceRefs name the CBA section to consult.
If a provision below is marked ESCALATE-IF-UNKNOWN and you cannot determine one of the facts it depends on, do not guess: the answer is that the question cannot be decided on the facts supplied.

SCOPE EXCLUSIONS
* The instance turns on a CBA provision family that this pack does not encode (listed in packs/COVERAGE.md, section 'Out of scope'). No legality answer may be produced from the encoded rules alone.
  Applies when: contains provision outside pack scope is true.
  Effect: escalate.

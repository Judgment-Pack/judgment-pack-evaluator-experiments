R1-1 — BLOCKER — `rule/SPEC.md` §§1, 3; `rule/transition.py:181-242`; `harness/run_verify.py:57-70,108-126`: The claimed layer separation is false and unsafe. `run_verify` extracts raw checkpoint payloads and invokes TRANSITION even when CURRENCY rejects the snapshot; `position-window` and `run-to-expiry` never inspect `currency_outcome`, `stop-at-retirement` maps every non-`pass` outcome—including `unavailable` and signature/chain failures—to “version retired,” and `combined` is solely TRANSITION’s result. Thus a snapshot with an invalid attestation can still produce combined `usable`, and a never-bound digest can receive `usable` from `position-window`. This contradicts the claim that TRANSITION consumes CURRENCY’s verdict “as a fact” without recomputing membership and bypasses Study 016’s authenticated verification ceremony (`studies/016-policy-currency-anchor/registry/verify_currency.py:390-479`). Fix: allow transition evaluation only after CURRENCY returns `pass` or exact `fail:not-current-at-snapshot`; return `transition-unavailable` for every integrity/unavailability result, preserve CURRENCY’s raw result independently, and add invalid-signature, invalid-chain, unavailable, and never-bound-digest controls proving none can combine to `usable`.
